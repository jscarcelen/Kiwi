What decides the date

Three things can slip the launch. Everything else can run in parallel.

1

Android is gated by a 14-day test

A new personal Google Play account needs a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days before it can go public. Start it in week 1 and Android is live by week 3.

2

Stripe live needs a legal entity

Payments, payouts and the business bank account all need the company and an EIN. Form it by week 3 so premium features can charge in week 6.

3

Apple: individual first, company later

An individual Apple account is approved in 1 to 3 days. A company account needs a D-U-N-S number and takes 7+ days. Ship on the individual account now, transfer the app to the company later.
